# ムレカ（Mureka）用 指示文 — ONE GOAL フルサイズ（約3分）

## ① スタイル（曲調）欄

```
Energetic J-rock stadium anthem for a school soccer tournament, inspired by the excitement of the FIFA World Cup. 140 BPM, 4/4, key of D major with a B minor verse. Powerful distorted electric guitars, driving bass, big tribal tom drums, hand claps, brass stabs. Stadium crowd chant "Oh oh-oh-oh" in the intro and outro. Unison group vocals of Japanese junior high school students, male and female, shouting with passion and team spirit. Full-length song, about 3 minutes. Structure: chant intro, verse, pre-chorus, chorus, guitar solo, second verse, chorus, quiet emotional bridge with piano, drum build-up, key change into a huge final chorus, crowd chant outro. Hot, heroic, uplifting sports anthem.
```

## メモ
- 「二中」は読み間違い防止のため、ひらがな「にちゅう」で記載。
- [Guitar Solo] [Build-up] などの見出しは、間奏や盛り上げ部分を作らせるためのもの（歌われない想定）。
- 短く仕上がった場合は作り直すか、[Guitar Solo] の後に [Instrumental Break] を足す。
